# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Meet eight illustrated cats that roam above your windows.
- Enjoy hand-drawn walking, blinking, breathing, sleeping, and stretching animations.
- Pet, drag, and personalize your companion from the menu bar.
- Choose cat size, walking speed, roaming area, and launch at login.
- Receive signed updates automatically through Sparkle, or choose Check for Updates from the menu.
- Supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later.
